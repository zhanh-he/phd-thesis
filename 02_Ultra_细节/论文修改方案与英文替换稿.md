## E03. 第一章统一 velocity 的概念，不能再写成普遍物理力度或响度

**位置与问题。** §1.2 “Symbolic editability” p.4／PDF31；§1.3、Table1.1 p.4／PDF31；§1.4 “ontological gap” p.5／PDF32。原 “universal loudness variable”、 “measurement of physical intensity”、 “initial strike force” 与本书后文的控制量/代理定义不一致。这是**我们的额外一致性核查**，与 R1-09 的协议边界相关。源码：[Intro.tex](/Users/jollibear/Documents/PhD_Thesis_Source_20261022/Chapter_1/Intro.tex:62)、[Background.tex](/Users/jollibear/Documents/PhD_Thesis_Source_20261022/Chapter_2/Background.tex:276)。

**§1.2 对应句建议替换。**

> MIDI velocity is an editable note-level control used in many synthesis workflows. Its effect on loudness and timbre depends on the instrument and renderer, so an equal velocity value does not imply an equal physical action or perceived loudness across instruments.

**§1.3 MIDI velocity 整段建议替换。**

> In this thesis, MIDI velocity denotes the note-onset control value assigned to a MIDI note event. In sensor-equipped piano datasets, this value is recorded from the instrument and provides a practical supervised target related to key or hammer motion. It is not a direct measurement of physical force or perceived loudness. In a synthesiser, velocity can affect amplitude, sample selection, and timbre according to the rendering configuration. The value is therefore precise within the MIDI representation, while its acoustic interpretation remains instrument- and renderer-dependent. The flat-velocity baseline used here assigns the value 64 to every note.

**Table1.1 对应单元格。** Nature 改为 “Note-level MIDI control value”；Conceptual Level 改为 “One attack-control value per supplied note event”。Score dynamics 的 Nature 可改 “Contextual symbolic instruction”，不以笼统 physical/cognitive 二分作精确学科定义。

**§1.4 对应段建议替换。**

> Dynamics recovery is not a deterministic label-conversion problem. Audio provides acoustic evidence related to perceived loudness; MIDI velocity is a note-level control value; score dynamics is a contextual symbolic instruction. These representations interact, but their relation depends on the instrument, renderer, musical context, and annotation convention. A method must therefore state which representation it predicts and what evidence supports that target.

**理由与状态。** 后文较谨慎的概念定义 **已由原文确认**：§2.1.1 p.10／PDF37，§2.3.2 p.19／PDF46，§6.3 p.88／PDF115，§6.9.1 pp.101–102／PDF128–129。这里不引入未经核实的 MIDI 协议细目。中文统一“MIDI 力度值／音符级 MIDI 控制值”。

## E06. 定义 weakly expressive、weakly supervised 与三种任务

**位置。** §1.5–1.6 pp.6–7／PDF33–34；§2.4.3 p.23／PDF50；§3.3 pp.40–41／PDF67–68；R1-05、19、23、27、37。

**建议定义段。**

> Here, weakly expressive data means that the dynamics layer is absent, flattened, or unreliable for the intended use; it does not mean that all aspects of musical expression are weak. Weakly supervised velocity estimation means that direct target-domain note-velocity labels are unavailable and learning instead uses an indirect, renderer-based acoustic objective. Full automatic music transcription additionally requires estimation of note content and timing, which these fixed-note evaluations do not assess.

## E08. Ch3 的物理时间、量化使用场景与风格范围

**物理时间说明建议替换/增补。**

> The implemented input uses physical time: each default 10-second segment is rasterised into 96 frames. ## E30. 结论、hairpins 与完整 AMT 的未来工作

**位置。** §7.2.1 p.108／PDF135，§7.3–7.5 pp.110–112／PDF137–139；§5.7.5 p.82／PDF109；R1-37、38、R2-01、03、04。

**§7.2.1 避免把Ch5推测为已知结构。**

> The studies use or estimate different structural coordinates. **§7.3拟增两个限制。**

> The symbolic-only results establish plausible conditional completion, not unique reconstruction of an absent performance. **§7.4.5拟增具体未来接口。**

> A complete expressive-AMT evaluation could combine a note-event transcriber, alignment or metrical estimation, and a dynamics module, then propagate uncertain or incorrect note events through the interface. It would need to measure note transcription and dynamics quality separately, as well as their combined usefulness. The present thesis demonstrates velocity gains at known notes and score-dynamics prediction under the stated protocol; it does not demonstrate improved note-transcription F1 from adding dynamics. **§7.5收尾建议替换。**

> This thesis contributes a set of methods and an account of their conditions of use. Symbolic completion, score-informed refinement, audio-only score-dynamics prediction, and proxy-supervised adaptation address different information constraints. Their common result is a principled way to define and evaluate a useful dynamics layer while keeping its interpretation tied to the available evidence. The studies support this scenario-based account; they do not constitute a single complete transcription system or a universal recovery of physical performance dynamics.
