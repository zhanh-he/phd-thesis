# A版｜研究路线故事版

> 按研究路线推进：先补、再修、再写回谱面，最后探索钢琴以外。
> 中文是完整口述稿，English逐段对应。选一种语言从01读到13即可；段号、语言标签和说明不念。
> 固定英文正文 **775词**，约 **5.74–6.46分钟**；另预留 **30–45秒** 讲你自己的爱好与课题缘起，合计约 **6.24–7.21分钟**。均为估计，未实测。

## 01

**中文**

大家好。今天我想讲的事情其实很简单：现在的自动音乐转录，已经能做不少关于“弹了哪些音、什么时候弹”的事情。但音符找出来以后，音乐的强弱这一层，常常还不完整。我的论文就是围绕这个痛点，做三个case、三个解决方案，最后再探索能不能走到钢琴以外。

**English**

Hello everyone. The idea behind my thesis is quite simple. Automatic music transcription can already do a lot with which notes were played and when. But once we have the notes, the dynamics can still be missing or unreliable. My thesis tackles this through three cases and three solutions, followed by an exploration beyond piano.

## 02

**中文**

先介绍一下，我是Zhanhong He，来自西澳大学。在讲论文之前，我想先从自己为什么会对这个问题感兴趣说起。这和我平时喜欢做的事情，以及我怎么开始看待音乐里的强弱，有一些关系。

**English**

First, I'm Zhanhong He, from the University of Western Australia. Before getting into the thesis, I'd like to share how I became interested in this problem. It connects with what I enjoy doing outside the research, and how I started thinking about dynamics in music.

> **此处停下来讲你的个人故事，30–45秒。** 爱好是什么、哪个具体经历让你开始在意音乐动态，由你自己讲。这里没有替你编经历。讲完后，直接接03的“回到研究／资料”。本提示不念。

## 03

**中文**

回到研究本身，我想利用的是我们已经有的东西。因为很多时候，音符或者录音并不缺，缺的是可以用的动态信息。那么我们能不能把这一层单独补出来？不需要每次都把整个转录问题重新做一遍。这是我这条研究路线的出发点。

**English**

Coming back to the research, I want to make use of what we already have. Often, the notes or recordings are available. What is missing is useful dynamics information. Can we fill in that part without starting the whole transcription problem again? That is where this research route begins.

## 04

**中文**

第一个case，我们手里只有MIDI音符。音高和时间都在，但力度值可能全都一样，或者根本不能用。这时直接播放，虽然音符没有少，却很难听出旋律和伴奏之间的轻重。我的第一个问题就是：只看这些音符本身，能不能学出一层比较合理的力度变化？

**English**

In the first case, we only have MIDI notes. Pitch and timing are available, but velocity may be flat or unusable. Playing the right notes at the same velocity can lose the balance between melody and accompaniment. So my first question is whether the note context itself can help us produce a reasonable pattern of velocity changes.

## 05

**中文**

我的解决方案是让模型学习音符上下文和力度之间的关系，再把力度补回这些已有音符上。它只动这一层，音符和时间不变。在钢琴数据上的比较中，预测误差比所比较的基线更低。我也用小规模听测，检查这种变化是不是真的听得出来。结果支持它在所选片段上的价值。不过，这里补的是一种合理演奏，不是唯一还原原来那一次演奏。

**English**

My solution learns a relationship between note context and velocity, then fills in the values at the existing notes. It changes that layer while keeping notes and timing fixed. On the piano datasets, it gives lower error than the comparison systems. A small listening study also supports an audible benefit in the selected excerpts. With notes alone, though, we are producing a plausible interpretation, not uniquely reconstructing the original performance.

## 06

**中文**

接下来，第二个case，我们多了一样东西：演奏音频，而且有对齐的谱面。既然能听到那次演奏，就不必只根据音符猜力度了。但我们也不用丢掉已经知道的音符结构。于是问题变成：能不能把谱面里这些现成信息用起来，帮助音频模型把力度估计得更好？

**English**

The second case adds something important: performance audio, together with an aligned score. Now we can hear the performance, rather than rely on notes alone. We also have note structure that we do not need to estimate again. The question becomes how to use that score information to improve the audio-based velocity estimate.

## 07

**中文**

这里我做了一个接在已有估计器上的修正模块。先由音频得到初始力度，再用谱面信息，在已知音符的位置做修正。我把它接到几种钢琴估计器上，也看了从MAESTRO到SMD的表现。所测比较显示，这样修正确实能改善力度估计。这项工作的重点是把已有谱面用好，也让同一种修正思路可以接到不同前端上。

**English**

I add a correction module to an existing estimator. The audio gives an initial velocity estimate, and score information helps refine it at known note positions. I test this with several piano estimators, including transfer from MAESTRO to SMD. The comparisons show improved velocity estimation in the tested settings. The practical point is to use the score we already have and make the correction work with different front ends.

## 08

**中文**

第三个case又不一样了。现在只有一段录音，没有对齐的音符；而我们想拿到的，是可以放在乐谱上的强弱标记，比如p、mf和f。那就不能一直沿用“给每个音符填一个MIDI力度值”的想法。我需要直接从音频判断动态状态，以及它在哪里发生变化。

**English**

The third case is different again. We have a recording, but no aligned notes, and we want dynamic markings that can go on a score: piano, mezzo forte, or forte. So assigning MIDI velocity to known notes is no longer the right task. I need to estimate the dynamic level directly from audio and find where it changes.

## 09

**中文**

我的解决方案是把动态等级、变化点、节拍和小节首拍一起预测。原因也很直接：除了知道强弱，还要知道标记应该放在哪里。模型既要看一段时间里的动态发展，也要抓准节拍的位置。所以这里采用共享模型，让这些相关任务一起学习，同时照顾不同的时间需求。

**English**

My solution predicts dynamic levels, change points, beats, and downbeats together. The reason is practical: we need both the marking and a musical position for it. The model has to follow dynamics across a passage while locating beats accurately. I use shared learning with different temporal scales to bring those needs together, so that the outputs describe both the dynamics and where it belongs.

## 10

**中文**

这一部分把研究从音符级的MIDI力度值，推进到了谱面上的动态描述。它们都和强弱有关，但服务的是不同用途。实验里的动态和变化点分数使用参考节拍，让我先单独评价动态这一部分。因此这里展示的是解决方案及其受测表现，还不是把完整自动乐谱生成全部做完了。

**English**

This takes the work from note-level MIDI velocity to score-level dynamics. Both concern musical strength, but they serve different uses. In the experiments, dynamics and change points are evaluated on reference beats, so I can examine that part separately. This establishes the task and its tested behaviour, rather than a complete automatic score-writing system.

## 11

**中文**

到这里，三个case都以钢琴为主。最后我再问一步：如果换成吉他，这条路线还能不能继续？困难在于，钢琴有比较好的直接力度标签，真实吉他录音却通常没有。音频和对齐音符可以在，但训练需要的力度目标缺了。所以最后这项探索，重点是没有直接标签时，怎样继续学习。

**English**

Up to this point, the three cases focus mainly on piano. My final question is whether we can take the work beyond piano, starting with guitar. Piano offers useful direct velocity labels; real guitar recordings generally do not. We may still have audio and aligned notes, but the target for supervised learning is missing. So the final exploration asks how learning can continue without those direct labels.

## 12

**中文**

我用一个可微的SoundFont代理，把预测力度和声音里的强弱描述连接起来。先让代理学会指定渲染器的响应，再固定代理，用真实录音来适配力度估计器。受测吉他结果中，重新合成后的响度轮廓与录音更接近，相关性也优于所比较的其他方案。这里得到的是适合指定渲染器的控制值，不是物理拨弦力，也不代表所有乐器都已经解决。

**English**

I use a differentiable SoundFont proxy to connect predicted velocity with loudness-related descriptions of sound. After learning the renderer's response, the proxy is fixed and helps adapt the estimator against real recordings. On the tested guitar data, resynthesised loudness contours agree better with the recordings than the compared alternatives. The output is a control for that renderer, not physical plucking force or a solution already established for every instrument.

## 13

**中文**

所以，我这篇论文的路线就是：只有音符时，补力度；音频加谱面时，修力度；只有音频、想写回乐谱时，预测动态标记。三个case分别处理当前流程里缺的那一块。最后，再探索缺少直接标签时能否扩展到钢琴以外。接下来我就沿着这条路线，展开介绍每一项工作。

**English**

So the route through my thesis is: complete velocity when we have notes; refine velocity when we have audio and a score; and predict dynamic markings when we have audio and want notation. These are three solutions to missing parts of the current workflow. Finally, I explore adaptation beyond piano when direct labels are unavailable. I will now follow that route through the individual studies.
