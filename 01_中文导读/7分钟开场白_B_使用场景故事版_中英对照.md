# B版｜使用场景故事版

> 从三个实际需要切入：手里有什么、想拿到什么、怎样把缺的一块补上。
> 中文是完整口述稿，English逐段对应。选一种语言从01读到13即可；段号、语言标签和说明不念。
> 固定英文正文 **792词**，约 **5.87–6.60分钟**；另预留 **30–45秒** 讲你自己的爱好与课题缘起，合计约 **6.37–7.35分钟**。均为估计，未实测。

## 01

**中文**

大家好。我这篇论文想处理的，是用AMT时很容易遇到的一件事：我们把音符转出来了，但动态这一层还没处理好。比如，MIDI能播放了，强弱却比较平；或者想要一份有强弱标记的谱面，系统给出的信息还不够。我围绕这件事做了三个case，最后再探索钢琴以外的情况。

**English**

Hello everyone. My thesis starts with a practical problem in using AMT: we can transcribe the notes, but the dynamics may still need work. A MIDI file might play correctly while sounding flat. Or we may want dynamic markings on a score that the system does not provide. I work through three cases around this problem, then explore what happens beyond piano.

## 02

**中文**

我是Zhanhong He，来自西澳大学。正式讲这三个case之前，我想先讲一下自己为什么会在意这个问题。这部分和我的爱好有关，也和我为什么会开始这个课题有关。我想先从这个比较个人的角度，把今天的故事带出来。

**English**

I'm Zhanhong He, from the University of Western Australia. Before introducing the three cases, I'd like to explain why I care about this problem. It connects with my interests outside the research and with how this project began. I want to start with that personal side, then bring it back to the work.

> **此处停下来讲你的个人故事，30–45秒。** 爱好是什么、哪个具体经历让你开始在意音乐动态，由你自己讲。这里没有替你编经历。讲完后，直接接03的“回到研究／资料”。本提示不念。

## 03

**中文**

好，那回到我们手里的资料。我想先问两个很实际的问题：现在有什么？最后想得到什么？同样是动态缺失，手里有MIDI、音频加谱面，或者只有录音，需要的解决方案并不一样。我的三个case，就是从这几种实际需要出发。

**English**

Now, coming back to the material we have, I ask two practical questions: what is available, and what do we want at the end? Missing dynamics does not always mean the same problem. Having MIDI, audio with a score, or a recording alone gives us different things to work with. The three cases follow those different needs.

## 04

**中文**

先看第一个。假设我已经有一份MIDI，音符和时间都没问题，但所有音符的力度一样。我要的不是再转录一遍音符，而是让已有音符之间有合理的轻重。比如哪些地方突出一点，哪些地方收一点，旋律和伴奏怎样拉开。这个case的任务就是MIDI力度补全。

**English**

For the first case, imagine that I already have a MIDI file. The notes and timing are fine, but every note has the same velocity. I do not need another transcription of the notes. I want useful variation between them: which notes come forward, which sit back, and how melody and accompaniment balance. This is the velocity-completion case.

## 05

**中文**

我让模型从音符上下文学习，再给这些位置补力度值。音符不改，时间也不改。钢琴数据上的比较显示，力度误差比所比较的基线更低；小规模听测也支持所选例子里的可听改善。这说明，只补动态这一层就有实际价值。但没有演奏音频，我们是在生成一种合理解释，不是在猜唯一正确的原演奏。

**English**

I train a model to use note context and fill in velocity at those positions. Notes and timing stay fixed. The piano comparisons show lower velocity error than the baselines, and a small listening study supports an audible improvement in the selected examples. So adding just this layer can be useful. Without performance audio, however, it is a plausible interpretation, not a uniquely correct reconstruction of an earlier performance.

## 06

**中文**

第二个case，换一种需要。假设我有演奏录音，也有对齐的谱面。我想知道的是这次演奏里的力度，而不只是从音符生成一个合理版本。音频估计器已经能给出初始结果，那我们能不能把谱面这个现成资源也用上，帮助它修得更好？

**English**

The second case has a different need. Suppose I have a performance recording and an aligned score. I want velocity for this performance, rather than a plausible version from notes alone. An audio estimator already gives us an initial answer. Can we also use the score as an available resource to improve that answer?

## 07

**中文**

这里的做法很直接：在原有力度估计器后面加一个修正模块，用谱面信息帮助调整音符起点上的力度。我把同一个思路接到几种钢琴估计器上，也看它们在不同数据集上的表现。受测结果显示，谱面修正能带来改善。这个方案的用处，就是已有前端可以继续用，已有谱面也没有浪费。

**English**

The solution is to add a correction module after the velocity estimator, using score information to adjust velocity at known note onsets. I apply this idea to several piano estimators and examine their behaviour across datasets. In the tested comparisons, score-informed correction improves the estimates. The useful part is that we can keep working with an existing front end while making use of the score, too.

## 08

**中文**

再看第三个case。我手里只有录音，但我想得到的是给人看的动态标记，比如这一段是p，后面变成f。这已经不是前两个case里“给已知音符填力度值”的任务了。我要知道动态是什么、什么时候变，以及标记应该落在哪个音乐位置。

**English**

Now take the third case. I only have a recording, but I want dynamics that a person can read on a score: this passage is piano, and later it becomes forte. That is different from filling velocity at known notes. I need the dynamic level, the points where it changes, and musical positions for those markings.

## 09

**中文**

所以我把动态等级、变化点、节拍和小节首拍一起预测。这样，模型既考虑强弱的发展，也考虑标记的位置。动态需要看较长一段音乐，节拍却要抓得很准，这也是为什么我在模型里结合不同时间尺度。实验中，动态分数使用参考节拍，先把动态预测这一部分单独看清楚。

**English**

I predict dynamic levels, change points, beats, and downbeats together. The model considers both how dynamics develops and where a marking belongs. Dynamics needs a broader view of a passage, while beats need precise timing, which is why I bring different temporal scales into the model. In the experiments, the dynamics scores use reference beats, letting me examine that part separately.

## 10

**中文**

这三个case也让我把两个容易混在一起的东西分开了。MIDI力度值是每个音符的控制值；谱面动态是给人看的、依赖音乐语境的指示。它们都和响度有关，但不能直接换算。因此，我并不是用一个输出硬套所有用途，而是先看当前缺什么，再做对应的补充。

**English**

These cases also separate two things that are easy to mix up. MIDI velocity is a control value for a note. Score dynamics is a contextual instruction for a musician. Both relate to loudness, but there is no universal conversion between them. So I do not force one output to serve every use. I start with what is missing and build the corresponding addition.

## 11

**中文**

然后我才把问题带到钢琴以外。如果换成吉他，最先卡住的可能还不是模型，而是没有直接力度标签。我们仍可能有录音和对齐音符，但没法照着钢琴的监督方式直接训练。我最后一项探索，就是看能不能通过声音里的信息，给估计器另一条学习路线。

**English**

Only then do I take the question beyond piano. With guitar, the first obstacle may be the lack of direct velocity labels. We can still have recordings and aligned notes, but cannot simply use the same direct supervision as for piano. The final exploration asks whether information in the sound can provide another way to adapt the estimator.

## 12

**中文**

我使用可微的SoundFont代理，先学习指定渲染器对音符和力度值的响应。然后固定代理，让真实录音里的响度相关信息引导估计器适配。在受测吉他数据上，重合成后的响度轮廓比所比较的其他方案更接近录音。这个结果说明这条路线值得探索；输出仍然是指定渲染器下的控制值，不是对真实物理力度的测量。

**English**

I use a differentiable SoundFont proxy that first learns a chosen renderer's response to notes and velocity. With the proxy fixed, loudness-related information from real recordings guides adaptation. On the tested guitar data, the resulting resynthesis follows the recorded loudness contours better than the compared alternatives. This supports exploring that route. The output remains a control for the chosen renderer, rather than a measurement of physical playing force.

## 13

**中文**

所以，如果用一句话串起我的论文，就是：AMT把音乐资料转出来以后，动态缺的这一块，怎样根据手里的条件补上？只有音符，就补力度；有音频和谱面，就修力度；只有音频、需要谱面表达，就预测动态标记。然后再探索，没有直接标签时能不能扩展到钢琴以外。这就是接下来我要展开讲的研究路线。

**English**

So the question running through my thesis is how to fill the dynamics gap using the material we have. With notes, complete velocity. With audio and a score, refine velocity. With audio and a notation goal, predict dynamic markings. Then explore whether we can move beyond piano without direct labels. Those are the three cases, their solutions, and the extension that I will walk through next.
